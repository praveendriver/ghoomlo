# Idea log

Every direction considered for Ghoomlo, newest first. Status is one of `untested`, `testing`, `supported`, `dropped`. Full reviews are in `idea-reviews/`.

| Date | Idea | Verdict | Riskiest assumption | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-16 | **School-holiday fare watch.** For families in one city, a weekly hand-curated WhatsApp digest of the best fares for upcoming school breaks and long weekends. | Promising and the cheapest to test; thin revenue is the worry. Founder's aim (17 Nov 2026): audience first via WhatsApp, not revenue. No open flight-price API found: Google Flights has none, Kayak and Skyscanner need partner approval; start hand-curated on a free WhatsApp Channel | Families will act on a curated alert (book, or ask for more) rather than just read it | testing: sign-up page and tracked links built 17 Nov 2026; thresholds not yet set |
| 2026-11-16 | **Tool for small travel agents.** Turn a customer enquiry into a branded day-by-day proposal page with maps, in minutes. | Mixed, leaning weak: crowded, and the incumbents already ship AI quote features (TravClan AI Quotes, Sembark AI Itinerary Builder and Importer, per their own pages, 17 Nov 2026) | There is a time-consuming step for small agents that those tools' AI does not cover, such as agents who use no tool at all | untested |
| 2026-11-16 | **Year-ahead planner.** For families, Ghoomlo takes the year's school dates, leave and budget, lays out the trips worth taking, watches prices and adjusts the plan. | Promising, with a hard dependency on price data | Families will plan months ahead and hand over their calendar, when most plan trip by trip | untested |
| 2026-11-16 | **Visual feed.** A picture-and-video-first experience with very little text that gathers the best vlog, blog and influencer content into categories for quick planning. | Mixed: attractive, but hard to defend and legally constrained | People want an organised set of clips more than they want the synthesis in words, and we can show the clips within platform rules | untested |
| 2026-11-16 | **Family-fit itinerary** (what is built). A day-by-day plan around food rules, ages and pace, with honest labels. | Weak as a reason to come; useful as a foundation | A tailored plan is enough to choose Ghoomlo over a free chatbot | testing (Maze study 01) |
| 2026-11-16 | **Dates and price watch.** Find the cheapest dates that fit school and work, and alert on drops. | Promising; folded into the year-ahead planner | People will act on an alert from a new, unknown product | testing (Maze study 01) |
| 2026-11-16 | **Vlog digest.** Summarise what travel vloggers agree and disagree on for a destination. | Promising; overlaps with the visual feed | A written digest saves enough time to replace watching | testing (Maze study 01) |

## Market watch

Outside moves that bear on which direction is worth testing. Facts are as reported in the linked coverage, not checked further.

| Date read | What happened | What it means for Ghoomlo |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-18 | **OpenAI bought Odessia**, an AI travel agent from Sonder's founder (about nine people, $6M pre-seed led by Sequoia), and shut the product the same day. Coverage ties it to travel inside ChatGPT, which already works with Booking.com, Expedia and KAYAK. ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/11/10/a-year-after-sonders-collapse-francis-davidson-sells-his-ai-travel-startup-to-openai/), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-acquires-odessia-ai-travel-agent)) | General "plan my trip with AI" is becoming a feature of ChatGPT. It is not a wedge for us. It also shows a small, sharp team can be acquired for its people and product. |
| 2026-11-18 | **Airbnb will launch its own AI travel agent** between January and June 2027, to explore, plan, book and manage whole trips, with an interface "much richer than a chatbot". ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/airbnb-ai-travel-agent-launch-2027), [Skift](https://skift.com/?p=840625)) | A second giant takes the rich, visual AI planner. That weakens the family-fit itinerary and the visual feed as reasons to come. |
| 2026-11-18 | **WeRoad raised $58M (Series C, led by Airbnb)** to take small-group trips for young travellers to the US. It reports 300,000+ past travellers and about €130M 2025 revenue. ([Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/05/27/weroad-lands-58m-to-scale-real-world-travel-experiences/), [Travolution](https://www.travolution.com/news/weroad-raises-58m-series-c-led-by-airbnb-for-us-expansion/)) | Money is going to a specific audience with a specific trip format and community, not to planning software. A narrow audience such as Indian school families is the sound shape. |
| 2026-11-18 | **"Voyage Labs raises $12M"** (TechCrunch, 17 Nov 2026) could not be confirmed: TechCrunch blocks this session and web search found no other coverage. | Not used as evidence until read. |

Overall: these moves strengthen the case for the school-holiday fare watch (local data the giants don't hold: Indian school calendars, long weekends, origin-city weather) and weaken any direction whose pitch is "AI plans your trip". A note for the exit idea: buyers so far bought teams and distribution, not plan generators.
