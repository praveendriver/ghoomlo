# Maze study 01 — what is worth building next?

**Decision this study informs:** which promise Ghoomlo should lead with: date-and-price help, a video digest, or family-fit plans.

**Who:** adults in India who planned a multi-day family leisure trip in the last 12 months.
**How many:** 25–30 completed responses for the questions; the first 5–8 are enough to spot problems in the website task.
**Length:** about 7 minutes. **Device:** mobile and desktop.
**Recruiting:** share the Maze link in school-parent, apartment and work WhatsApp groups. Aim for at least three home cities.

Questions about the last trip come before any mention of our ideas, so answers are not steered.

## Blocks, in order

| # | Maze block | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | "We are building a trip-planning tool for Indian families. This takes about 7 minutes. There are no right answers; we want to hear how you really plan." |
| 2 | Multiple choice (screener) | "In the last 12 months, did you plan a family trip of 3 days or more?" — Yes, I did most of the planning / Yes, I helped / No. *End the study on "No".* |
| 3 | Multiple choice (multi-select) | "Who travelled?" — Partner / Children under 6 / Children 6–17 / Parents or in-laws / Friends / Other |
| 4 | Open question | "Think about that trip. Which part of the planning took you the longest? Tell us what you did." |
| 5 | Multiple choice (pick up to 2) | "Which of these were the hardest?" — Finding dates that worked around school and work / Getting a good price on flights or hotels / Knowing when to book / Deciding what to see and do there / Building the day-by-day plan / Finding food that suits us / Visas and documents / None of these |
| 6 | Open question | "Which apps, sites or people did you use to plan it?" |
| 7 | Multiple choice | "Did you watch travel videos (YouTube, Instagram) while planning?" — Yes, many / A few / No |
| 8 | Ranking | "Rank these from most to least useful for your next trip." — **A.** Tell us your school and work dates; we find the cheapest dates that work and alert you when the price drops. / **B.** We watch the travel vlogs for your destination and tell you what they agree on and where they disagree. / **C.** A day-by-day plan built around your family: food rules, children's and grandparents' needs, and your pace. |
| 9 | Opinion scale (1–5) | "How likely would you be to use your top choice for your next trip?" (Not at all → Definitely) |
| 10 | Open question | "What would make your top choice not worth using?" |
| 11 | Website test | Start URL: the live Ghoomlo site. Task: "You want a relaxed 4-day family trip to Goa. Use this site to get a plan started. Stop when you can see a day-by-day plan." Success: reaches the Goa sample-plan page or a saved trip. |
| 12 | Opinion scale (1–5) | "How clear was it what this site does?" (Not clear → Very clear) |
| 13 | Open question | "What did you expect that was missing or confusing?" |
| 14 | Open question (optional) | "May we call you for 15 minutes to hear more? If yes, leave a WhatsApp number or email." |
| 15 | Thank you | "Thank you. Your answers shape what we build next." |

## How to read the results

- **Blocks 4–5** show the real pain. If under a third pick price or dates, lead elsewhere.
- **Block 8** gives the ranking; **block 9** shows whether the winner is wanted or merely preferred. Treat an average under 3.5 as weak.
- **Block 7 with block 8:** if people who watch many videos do not rank B first, the video digest is not the wedge.
- **Block 11:** watch where people first tap and whether they sign up or leave at the sign-up screen.

Decide the thresholds above before looking at responses.

## Before sending

- Replace the start URL in block 11 with the live site address.
- The website test needs Maze's snippet or browser extension on the site; follow Maze's prompt when adding the block.
- Block 14 collects contact details: say how they will be used, and delete them after the calls.
- Send to three people first and fix anything they stumble on.
