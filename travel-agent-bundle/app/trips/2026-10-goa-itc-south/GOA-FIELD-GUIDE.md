# PART 3 — EATING VEGETARIAN (the real challenge in Goa)

## The hidden-fish problem
Goan cuisine is built on **fish, prawn and pork**. Even "vegetable" dishes can hide **fish stock, dried fish, or shrimp (balchão) paste**, and beach-shack kitchens share woks. Baked goods and some breads can contain **egg**. So: **always show the veg card, and always say "no fish stock, no egg."**

## Show this at every restaurant (also on the Cheat Card)
```
We are PURE VEGETARIAN. No meat, no fish, no seafood/prawn, NO EGG.
No fish stock or shrimp paste in the gravy, please.
Milk, cheese, paneer, butter are OK. Is this dish vegetarian?
```

- **Beach shacks** are seafood-first with shared kitchens — treat as snack/drinks only unless they clearly cook veg separately.

# PART 6 — 🧠 HARD-WON GOTCHAS

## ⛩️ Temples
- **Dress code**: covered shoulders & knees; **shoes off** before entering. Keep a stole handy.
- **Midday break**: many Goan temples close for a few midday hours — go **before ~12:30** or confirm the day's timing.
- **⚠️ Foreigner-entry rule** at Shantadurga (Kavlem) & Mahalasa (Mardol) — see Day 3; we anchor on **Mangueshi** (no such rule) and call ahead for the others.
- **⛔ Don't eat at "Shantadurga Bhojanalay" by Mangueshi** — it serves fish.

## ⛪ Old Goa churches
- **Modest clothing**; it's an active place of worship. **Visit around Mass times** at the Basilica (ask concierge).
- Lots of **open, shadeless walking** and **little food at the site** — carry water; eat right after at The Rasoda.
- Keep valuables close; it's a busy tourist cluster.

## 💦 Dudhsagar
- **Jeep only.** **⛔ Walking the railway track to the falls is banned and fined** by the Forest Dept; getting on/off at the Dudhsagar railway stop is an offence. Don't improvise.
- The jeep track is **bumpy**; there's a **short walk + stream crossing** at the end — hence the change of clothes.
- **Food is sparse out there** — carry a hotel-packed veg lunch/snacks.

## 🚕 Cars & cash
- Goa taxis are **unmetered** — agree the fare/route first; the hotel car avoids the haggling.
- **Cash-first** at temples, church tickets, the jeep, and small veg eateries.

## 🏖️ Beaches & shacks
- Shacks are **seafood-first with shared kitchens** — snacks/drinks only unless veg is cooked separately.
- Swim only on **flagged, lifeguarded** stretches.
