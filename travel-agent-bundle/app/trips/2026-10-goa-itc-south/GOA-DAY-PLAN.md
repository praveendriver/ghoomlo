## Day 2 — Sat 17 Oct · History day — Old Goa churches + Panjim / Fontainhas

**Plain point:** Goa's UNESCO heart in the morning, the pretty Portuguese Latin Quarter in the afternoon. Modest clothing for the churches (covered shoulders/knees).

**Morning — Old Goa (Velha Goa), ~45–50 min from Arossim (⚠️ drive time estimated):**
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus** ✅ — holds **St Francis Xavier's remains** in a silver urn (begun 1594). The single most important church in Goa. ⚠️ Mass times not machine-verified; **don't use `bomjesus.org`** (it now redirects to an unrelated site) — use `stfrancisxavierofficial.com` or ask the concierge.
- **Sé Cathedral** ✅ — the largest church the Portuguese built in Asia (91 m), with the "Golden Bell."
- **Church of St Francis of Assisi** ✅ + the **Archaeological Museum** alongside it.
- **Church of St Cajetan** ✅ (modelled on St Peter's, Rome) and **Our Lady of the Rosary** ✅ (oldest surviving church here, 1543) if the kids are still game.
- **Kid note:** lots of walking, little shade, limited food right at the site — so we eat right after.

**Lunch — pinned, verified pure-veg:**
- **The Rasoda, Old Goa** ✅ VERIFIED-OFFICIAL — pure-veg, two floors, North Indian/Rajasthani, **right by the churches** (off NH-748). No hours online → call **+91 84477 43753**.
- Backup: **Udupi Pure Veg, Ella** (South Indian, ~1.3 km from Bom Jesus) ⚠️ listing only.

**Afternoon — Panjim + Fontainhas (~15 min from Old Goa):**
- **Fontainhas (Latin Quarter)** ✅ — Panjim's photogenic Portuguese quarter: coloured houses, narrow lanes, chapels. Lovely slow walk.
- **Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception** church (the big white staircase church above Panjim) + the riverfront.
- Snack: **Café Tato** or **Café Bhonsle** ⚠️ — old-school Goan **vegetarian** cafés (bhaji-puri, buns, samosas). Café Tato is near the Municipal Garden.
- Back to ITC (~45–60 min). Dinner at the resort or a nearby pure-veg (**The Veggies Pure Veg, Utorda** ⚠️, next to the hotel beach).

## Day 3 — Sun 18 Oct · Temple + nature day — Ponda temples + spice farm

**Plain point:** Goa's living Hindu temples in the morning, a kid-friendly spice-plantation walk + thali for lunch, slow afternoon back at the resort.

**⚠️ IMPORTANT — foreigner-entry flag (affects your US-passport child):** Wikipedia reports **Shri Shantadurga (Kavlem)** and **Shri Mahalasa (Mardol)** have **barred foreigner entry**. It's unclear whether this covers a child of Indian origin on a US passport, or whether it's enforced. So I've anchored the day on temples **without** that restriction and marked the other two "call ahead."

**Morning — Ponda cluster (~45–60 min from Arossim, ⚠️ estimated):**
- **Shri Mangueshi Temple (Priol)** ✅ — Shiva temple with a seven-storey lamp tower, sacred tank, Nandi bull. **Dress code** (covered shoulders/knees) but **no foreigner ban**. Great for kids (tank, lamp tower). *This is the anchor.*
- Nearby in **Bandora** (~4 km): **Shri Naguesh** ✅ (Ramayana wood carvings) and **Shri Mahalaxmi** ✅ (rare wooden Vishnu images) — easy add-ons.
- **Shri Shantadurga (Kavlem)** / **Shri Mahalasa (Mardol)** — ⚠️ **call ahead re: foreigner entry** before relying on these.
- Temple prasad is vegetarian — a nice safe treat.

**⛔ FOOD TRAP:** **"Shantadurga Bhojanalay"** next to Mangueshi Temple **serves a fish plate** — it is NOT the temple canteen. Don't be fooled by the name.

**Lunch — LOCKED: pure-veg, zero risk.** **Navtara, Ponda** ✅ VERIFIED-OFFICIAL (100% veg, opp. Ponda Municipal Garden). We do the spice-plantation **walk only** (skip the mixed buffet) so there's no fish-on-the-line risk.

**Afternoon — LOCKED:**
- **Ancestral Goa / Big Foot, Loutolim** ✅ (~25–30 min, closest attraction to the hotel) — a recreated old-Goa village + handicrafts; **the best single stop for the kids**. ⚠️ hours unverified → concierge.
- Then slow time at the ITC pool / Arossim beach. It's a Sunday — keep the evening gentle; Day 4 is an early start.

## Day 4 — Mon 19 Oct · The beautiful day — Dudhsagar Falls + Tambdi Surla

**Plain point:** Goa's spectacular 300 m waterfall at near-peak October flow, reached by a bumpy jeep safari — the kids will love it. Long day, early start.

- **Early departure**, ~1h15–1h45 to **Collem (Kulem)** ⚠️. Hire a **4WD jeep** there (local association) for the ~10 km forest track to **Dudhsagar Falls** ✅. ⚠️ Jeep price, booking and this week's running status → **concierge to confirm** (official sites were unreachable).
- **Do NOT walk the railway track** to the falls — it's banned by the Forest Dept and fined ✅. Jeep only.
- **Kid kit:** change of clothes, non-slip shoes, water, snacks — there's a short walk + a stream crossing at the end.
- Pair with **Tambdi Surla Mahadev Temple** ✅ nearby — a 12th-century stone temple, the oldest in Goa, quiet and beautiful (plausible add-on to the Dudhsagar/Mollem day).
- **Food out here is sparse** → I'll have the hotel **pack a veg lunch/snacks**, or we eat pure-veg on the way back via Ponda (**Navtara, Ponda** ✅). Flagging now so no one is stranded hungry.
- Back to the resort in the evening — a hot shower and an early dinner.
