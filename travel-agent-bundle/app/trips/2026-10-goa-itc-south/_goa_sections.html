Field guide
October 2026 · Dussehra break · South Goa

Temples, Old Goa,
and a waterfall.

Five nights at ITC Grand Goa in Arossim, used as a calm base. Four sightseeing days — the UNESCO churches of Old Goa with Panjim's Latin Quarter, the living Hindu temples around Ponda, the Dudhsagar waterfall by jeep at near-peak October flow, and a North Goa day for the famous Bollywood forts. A late-arrival Friday and a slow beach morning bracket it. Strictly lacto-vegetarian throughout, kid-paced.

The timeline

Four full sightseeing days from a single base at ITC Grand Goa, Arossim. All drive times are estimates (EST) to confirm with the concierge. Most temple/church hours could not be machine-verified, so they are flagged ⚠️ confirm rather than invented. Strictly lacto-vegetarian — no meat, fish, seafood or egg. Mid-October: monsoon just withdrawn, countryside green, Dudhsagar near peak flow, warm & humid with the odd short shower.

5 nights · Fri Oct 16 → Wed Oct 21, 2026 · Family of 4 · BLR origin · Vegetarian only · 5★ Bonvoy
Fri · Oct 16
Fly in to Dabolim → ITC Grand Goa, Arossim
Arrival No sightseeing
Evening
Kids finish school → evening BLR → GOI flight — a departure that clears school comfortably (sized at booking). About 1h in the air. No school missed.
On landing
Dabolim (GOI) → ITC Grand Goa · ~15–20 min EST — one of the closest 5-stars to the airport. Pre-book the hotel car with the concierge.
ITC Grand Goa
Check-in
Check in on Bonvoy Titanium — ask about suite upgrade, late checkout and lounge access. ⚠️ No pure-veg claim online — ask for separate veg preparation, no egg, and whether a Jain/veg-only option exists.
Dinner
Dinner at the resort — safest first-night bets are the 24-hour all-day venue and the Indian restaurant. Say “no fish stock, no egg.”
Sat · Oct 17
Old Goa churches + Panjim's Latin Quarter
History UNESCO Modest dress
Morning
Basilica of Bom Jesus + Sé Cathedral · Old Goa (~45–60 min EST) — the Basilica holds St Francis Xavier's remains (begun 1594); Sé Cathedral is the largest church the Portuguese built in Asia, with the “Golden Bell.” ⚠️ Mass times not verified — don't use bomjesus.org (redirects to an unrelated site); use stfrancisxavierofficial.com or ask the concierge.
Bom Jesus Sé Cathedral
Late morning
St Francis of Assisi + Archaeological Museum — and St Cajetan (modelled on St Peter's, Rome) + Our Lady of the Rosary if the kids are still game. Lots of walking, little shade — eat right after.
Lunch
The Rasoda, Old Goa · ✅ pure-veg — two floors, North Indian/Rajasthani, right by the churches. No hours online → call +91 84477 43753.
The Rasoda
Afternoon
Fontainhas & Panjim riverfront (~15 min from Old Goa) — Panjim's photogenic Portuguese quarter: coloured houses, narrow lanes, chapels, and Our Lady of the Immaculate Conception above the city. A lovely slow walk.
Fontainhas
Snack
Café Tato / Café Bhonsle · Goan veg cafés ⚠️ confirm — old-school bhaji-puri, buns, samosas near the Municipal Garden. Then back to ITC (~45–60 min EST).
Sun · Oct 18
Ponda temples + spice-farm walk + Loutolim
Temples Nature Kids
Morning
Shri Mangueshi Temple, Priol (~45–60 min EST) — the anchor — Shiva temple with a seven-storey lamp tower, sacred tank and Nandi bull. Dress code (covered shoulders/knees) but no foreigner ban. Great for kids. Nearby Shri Naguesh & Shri Mahalaxmi (Bandora) are easy add-ons.
Mangueshi
Note
⚠️ Foreigner-entry flag (your US-passport child) — Shri Shantadurga (Kavlem) & Shri Mahalasa (Mardol) reportedly bar foreigner entry. Call ahead before relying on them. ⛔ Trap: “Shantadurga Bhojanalay” next to Mangueshi serves a fish plate — it is NOT the temple canteen.
Lunch
Navtara, Ponda · ✅ 100% veg (locked) — opposite Ponda Municipal Garden. We do the spice-plantation walk only (skip the mixed buffet) so there's zero fish-on-the-line risk.
Navtara
Afternoon
Ancestral Goa / Big Foot, Loutolim (~25–30 min — closest attraction to the hotel) — a recreated old-Goa village + handicrafts; the best single stop for the kids. ⚠️ hours unverified → concierge. Then slow time at the ITC pool / Arossim beach.
Ancestral Goa
Mon · Oct 19
Dudhsagar Falls jeep safari + Tambdi Surla
Waterfall Early start Long day
Early
Depart to Collem (Kulem) · ~1h15–1h45 EST — hire a 4WD jeep there (local association) for the ~10 km forest track to the falls. ⚠️ Jeep price, booking and this week's running status → concierge to confirm.
Falls
Dudhsagar Falls · 300 m, near-peak October flow — a short walk + a stream crossing at the end. ⛔ Do NOT walk the railway track — banned by the Forest Dept and fined. Jeep only. Kid kit: change of clothes, non-slip shoes, water, snacks.
Dudhsagar
Pair with
Tambdi Surla Mahadev Temple — a 12th-century stone temple, the oldest in Goa, quiet and beautiful (plausible add-on to the Dudhsagar/Mollem day).
Tambdi Surla
Food
Food out here is sparse — have the hotel pack a veg lunch/snacks, or eat pure-veg on the way back via Ponda (Navtara ✅). Flagged so no one is stranded hungry. Back to the resort for a hot shower and an early dinner.
Tue · Oct 20
North Goa: Chapora Fort (Dil Chahta Hai) + Fort Aguada + souvenirs
Bollywood Long drive Shopping
Note
Oct 20 is Dussehra (Vijayadashami), a Goa public holiday — a festival day out and about. ⚠️ Shops and markets may close or keep short hours — have the concierge check before you set out. (Some calendars put Dussehra on Oct 21 instead.)
Reality
A long-drive day — North Goa is ~1h15–1h30 each way EST from Arossim. Leave right after an early breakfast. Prefer not to drive ~3 hrs round-trip with the kids? Swap it for a calmer South-Goa day: Cabo de Rama clifftop fort (~1h–1h15 EST) + Palolem Beach (~45 min EST).
Morning
Chapora Fort — the Dil Chahta Hai fort · ✅ confirmed — the hilltop ramparts from the 2001 film, with wide views over Vagator and the Chapora river. A short uphill walk; the kids will clock it as “the fort from the poster.” ⚠️ Opening hours (~9:30–17:30) & free entry come from tourism portals, not an official notice — treat as unconfirmed.
Chapora Fort
Below
Vagator Beach — the cove under the fort — the beach Chapora looks down on. ⚠️ Mid-October: the season is only just opening — few shacks, quiet sand, swimming may be rough. A photo-and-paddle stop more than a swim.
Midday
Fort Aguada & lighthouse, Sinquerim — a 17th-century Portuguese sea fort that's appeared in Dhoom, Dil Chahta Hai and Rangeela (and the Golmaal films) — ⚠️ film credits per Hindustan Times/Holidify, not an official list. Big ramparts, a lighthouse, Arabian-Sea views. ⚠️ hours/fee unverified → concierge.
Fort Aguada
Shopping
Souvenirs & handicrafts · Calangute / Candolim — shops and the Tibetan market near the forts: spices, cashews, feni, Goan handicrafts, beachwear. ⛔ The famous flea markets are NOT on today — Anjuna runs Wednesdays, Arpora's night market Saturdays, and neither falls on this Tuesday (the season has barely started). ⚠️ confirm shop hours — it's Dussehra.
Late lunch
Veg lunch on the drive back · Panjim — break the journey south at Panjim for pure-veg (Café Tato ⚠️ confirm, already on your veg list). Ask the driver for a shuddh shakahari place; say “no fish, no egg.” Then the long drive back to Arossim and an early resort dinner.
Wed · Oct 21
Slow morning at Arossim → afternoon flight home
Beach Departure
Morning
Lazy breakfast · Arossim / Utorda beach & pool — the resort's own calm South-Goa stretch. No rushing on the last morning.
Optional
A quick Colva Beach stop (South Goa, close to the hotel) — if there's time before checkout: Colva is where Shah Rukh Khan's Kabhi Haan Kabhi Naa was filmed — a short, close south-Goa film tie-in. ⚠️ keep it short — afternoon flight.
Colva Beach
Checkout
Late checkout (Titanium 4 pm helps) → GOI · 15–20 min — lunch at the resort or The Veggies Pure Veg, Utorda, then the short transfer to the airport for the afternoon flight home.

Eating safely

Verified vegetarian anchors to carry

Strict lacto-vegetarian — no meat, fish, seafood or egg; dairy and tofu are fine. Goan “veg” gravies can hide fish/prawn paste and baked goods can contain egg, so always say “no fish stock, no egg.” These are the vetted anchors; the full list with dish names is in the field guide.
