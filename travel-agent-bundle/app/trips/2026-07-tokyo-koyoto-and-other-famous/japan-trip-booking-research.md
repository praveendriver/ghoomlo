## 🥗 VEGETARIAN NOTES

**Challenges:**
- Japanese cuisine heavily uses dashi (fish stock) even in "vegetarian" dishes
- Soy sauce may contain fish extracts
- Limited awareness of strict vegetarian diet

**Strategies:**
1. **Learn key phrases:**
   - "Watashi wa bejitarian desu" (I am vegetarian)
   - "Sakana mo niku mo taberaremasen" (I cannot eat fish or meat)
   - "Tamago wa daijoubu desu ka?" (Is egg okay?) - if lacto-ovo vegetarian

2. **Safe Options:**
   - Convenience stores (Family Mart, 7-Eleven): onigiri with umeboshi/konbu, vegetable sandwiches (check labels)
   - Indian restaurants in Tokyo/Osaka
   - International chains: McDonald's (veggie options limited), Mos Burger (has veggie patty)
   - Hotel breakfasts (Western buffets)

3. **Restaurant Apps:**
   - HappyCow app - vegetarian restaurant finder
   - Google Translate camera feature for menus

4. **Pack Snacks:** 
   - Bring protein bars, nuts, dried fruit for the 6yo
   - Japanese shops: bananas, bread, packaged snacks
