#### Oversized Baggage Rules (CRITICAL)

**From JR Central official site (verified):**

https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/info/oversized-baggage/
(verified — JR Central official oversized baggage information page)

**Rule:** Bags with total dimensions (length + width + height) **over 160cm require advance reservation** of "seats with oversized baggage area."

**Check your suitcases:** Measure each bag. Example: 75cm long + 50cm wide + 30cm tall = 155cm (OK, no reservation needed) vs 80+55+30 = 165cm (NEEDS reservation).

**If you need oversized baggage space:**
- Reserve "seat with oversized baggage area" when buying ticket
- **NO EXTRA CHARGE** for the reservation
- Storage area is **behind the last row of each car**, shared by the 5 passengers in that back row
- If you board WITHOUT reservation for oversized bags: ¥1,000 fine per bag

**If traveling WITH oversized bags without reservation:** Conductor will charge ¥1,000 and assign storage location

**For 4 large suitcases:**
- If 2+ bags exceed 160cm, book back-row seats for your family
- Otherwise use overhead racks, footwell space, or deck areas (between cars)
- **Alternative:** Send 2 bags ahead via Yamato (see Luggage Forwarding section)

#### Mt. Fuji Viewing — Which Seats?

⚠ **COULD NOT DEFINITIVELY VERIFY** seat letter orientation for Mt. Fuji views.

**General guidance from multiple sources:**
- Shinkansen seats are arranged in 2-3 configuration (typically AB-C and D-E)
- All seats rotate 180° so passengers can face forward
- Mt. Fuji is visible between Tokyo and Kyoto, weather permitting
- **Common advice:** "E seats" are often cited for Fuji views Tokyo→Kyoto, but this varies by car orientation

**Action needed:** ASK WHEN BOOKING. Station staff know which side faces Fuji. Request "Fuji-side window seats" for your family.

**Visibility note:** Fuji is only visible for ~15-20 minutes of the journey, between Shin-Yokohama and Shizuoka, and ONLY in clear weather. Don't make it the primary booking criterion.

#### If You Miss Your Reserved Train — CONTINGENCY

**From Seat61.com (verified):**

With a reserved seat ticket:
- **No penalty** for missing your specific train
- You can board the next Nozomi/Hikari/Kodama on the same route
- **Options:**
  1. Take the next train in an **unreserved car** (non-reserved cars available on most trains)
  2. Go to ticket office to change reservation to a later train (usually free if done before original departure)

**Frequency advantage:** With 4+ Nozomi per hour, the "next train" is typically 15 minutes away.

**EXCEPTION:** During major holidays (New Year, Golden Week, Obon), Nozomi may be "temporarily fully reserved" with no unreserved cars.

**Your arrival day (Jul 7) is NOT a major holiday**, so unreserved cars should be available.

## If You Miss a Train — Contingency Guide

### Shinkansen Reservation Flexibility

**From verified sources (Seat61.com):**

#### Scenario 1: You Have Reserved Seats, Miss Your Train

**You can:**
1. **Board the next train in unreserved cars**
   - Most Tokaido Shinkansen trains have unreserved cars (typically cars 1-3)
   - No additional fee
   - First-come seating; may need to stand if full
   - EXCEPTION: During major holidays (New Year, Golden Week, Obon), some Nozomi trains are fully reserved
   
2. **Change your reservation at ticket office**
   - Go to "midori-no-madoguchi" (ticket office)
   - Usually free if done before original train's departure
   - They can book you on next available train with reserved seats

3. **Change via Smart-EX app** (if you booked online)
   - Smart-EX typically allows changes up to departure time
   - ⚠ COULD NOT VERIFY exact Smart-EX change policy

**No penalty fee** for missing your specific reserved train in most cases.

#### Scenario 2: How Often Is "The Next Train"?

**Nozomi frequency:** 4+ per hour = every 15 minutes
**Hikari frequency:** 2 per hour = every 30 minutes

**Practical impact:** If you miss your 10:00 Nozomi, the next Nozomi is likely 10:15, next after that 10:30.

**Combined service:** Nozomi + Hikari + Kodama = trains departing "every few minutes" (verified from Japan-Guide.com)

#### Scenario 3: Navigating Tokyo Station

⚠ **COULD NOT VERIFY** detailed Tokyo Station navigation during research.

**General guidance:**
- Tokyo Station is LARGE and can be confusing
- **Follow bilingual signs** to "Shinkansen" (新幹線)
- **Two sets of gates:** 
  1. Regular JR gates (your Suica/IC card or regular ticket)
  2. Shinkansen-specific gates (your Shinkansen ticket)
- **Tokaido Shinkansen platforms** are separate from other lines
- Allow **30-45 minutes** for first-time navigation with luggage
- **Signs differentiate:** Nozomi / Hikari / Kodama by platform/track

**Ask station staff:** "Tokaido Shinkansen, Kyoto-yuki" (bound for Kyoto) — they will point you to correct platform.

#### Scenario 4: Arriving Late at Night

**Your arrival (05:00):** No night-arrival concerns; trains run frequently by 07:00-08:00.

**Evening travel:** Last Nozomi from Tokyo to Kyoto is typically around 21:30-22:00. If you miss late trains, options shrink rapidly.

## Navigation & Reading the Stations

### Key Japanese Signs (Romanized)

**At stations, look for:**
- **新幹線** (Shinkansen) — Bullet train
- **のぞみ** (Nozomi) — Fastest service
- **ひかり** (Hikari) — Next fastest
- **こだま** (Kodama) — All-stations service
- **京都** (Kyoto)
- **東京** (Tokyo)
- **改札** (Kaisatsu) — Ticket gates
- **切符売り場** (Kippu uriba) — Ticket office
- **みどりの窓口** (Midori-no-madoguchi) — JR ticket office (green sign)

### Platform Identification

- **Track numbers** are posted in large digits
- **Train type** (Nozomi/Hikari/Kodama) is posted on electronic boards
- **Destination** and **departure time** are on the same boards
- **Car numbers** are marked on the platform floor (so you board the correct car for your seat)

### Staff Assistance

English-speaking staff are available at:
- Tokyo Station ticket offices
- Kyoto Station ticket offices
- Shinagawa Station (if you use that route)
- Major city stations

**Useful phrase:** "Eigo o hanasemasu ka?" (Do you speak English?) — but most staff at major stations will recognize you and switch to English automatically.
