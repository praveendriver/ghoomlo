# Apps to Download Before You Fly

## Navigation & Transit

**Google Maps** — Essential for transit directions with real-time updates, train platform info, and fare estimates. Download OFFLINE maps for Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka regions before flying (Settings → Offline maps → Select area). Works perfectly for Japan's transit system with station names in English.
(verified)
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-maps/id585027354
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.maps

**Japan Travel by NAVITIME** — Best transit routing app specifically for foreign travelers. Shows exact train/platform/fare info, includes station facility details, and works better than Google Maps for complex transfers. Free basic version available.
⚠ COULD NOT VERIFY current 2026 App Store link - search "Japan Travel NAVITIME" in your app store
Alternative: **Jorudan** or **Hyperdia** (both have English transit routing)

## Translation & Language

**Google Translate** — CRITICAL for reading menus, signs, and basic conversation. Before flying: download the OFFLINE Japanese language pack (Settings → Offline translation → Download Japanese). Camera translation works offline once pack is downloaded - point at menus/signs for instant translation. Conversation mode for basic interactions.
(verified)
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google-translate/id414706506
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.translate

## Money & Connectivity

**XE Currency Converter** — Live exchange rates for JPY ↔ INR. Note: Offline rates NOT confirmed - update rates before losing connectivity each day. Most useful for quick price checks while shopping.
(verified)
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/xe-currency-converter/id315241195
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xe.currency

**Suica in Apple Wallet** (iPhone only) — Digital IC card for trains, buses, convenience stores, vending machines. Setup BEFORE flying: open Wallet → Add card → Transit card → Suica → fund with Apple Pay. Works offline once loaded. Android users: get physical Suica card at any station IC card machine on arrival.
https://support.apple.com/en-us/108961

## Do These 5 Things the Night Before You Fly

1. **Download offline maps** — Google Maps: Settings → Offline maps → "Tokyo" + "Kyoto" + "Osaka" (select custom areas covering your hotel neighborhoods + major stations)

2. **Download offline Japanese language pack** — Google Translate: Settings → Offline translation → Download Japanese (~50MB, works for camera + text translation without internet)

3. **Add Suica to Apple Wallet** (iPhone users) — Wallet app → + → Transit Card → Suica → fund ¥5000-10000 with Apple Pay. Android users: buy physical card at Kansai Airport IC card machine on arrival.

4. **Pre-fill Visit Japan Web** — https://www.vjw.digital.go.jp/ — Complete customs/immigration forms for all 4 family members. Screenshot QR codes. Saves 20-30 min at Kansai Airport immigration.

5. **Screenshot ALL confirmations** — Hotel confirmations, flight tickets, JR Pass exchange order, USJ tickets, DisneySea tickets, travel insurance. Save to Photos album "Japan Trip 2026" for offline access.

- **Google Maps**: Save key locations (hotels, train stations, restaurants) with stars/favorites for quick offline access
- **WhatsApp**: Test live location sharing with family before departure
- **Windy**: Check Mt Fuji area forecast for July 14-16 window

## Notes on App Store Region Restrictions

Some Japan-specific apps (Tokyo Disney Resort, USJ) may be region-locked or unavailable in Indian App Store. If you cannot find them:
- Try searching with full official name in English
- Download after arrival using hotel WiFi (apps detect you're in Japan and become available)
- Create secondary Japan region Apple ID if absolutely needed (not recommended - most apps work without this)

Most critical apps (Google Maps, Google Translate, GO taxi, Marriott, WhatsApp, Windy) are verified available globally.
