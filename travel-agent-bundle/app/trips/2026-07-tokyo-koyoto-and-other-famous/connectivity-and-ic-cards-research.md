## VERIFIED: Welcome Suica (Physical Tourist Card)

**What it is:**
- Tourist-only IC card, no deposit required (regular Suica has ¥500 refundable deposit)
- **Validity:** 28 days from first use
- **No refunds:** You cannot get remaining balance back (unlike regular Suica)
- Comes in physical card form OR app form ("Welcome Suica Mobile" for iPhone)

**Where to buy at Haneda Airport:**
- Available at Haneda and Narita airports (per japan-guide.com)
- ⚠ **EXACT LOCATION/COUNTER AT HANEDA NOT VERIFIED** — likely at Tourist Information desk (Terminal 3, 2F Arrivals) or IC card vending machines near station entrance
- Your flight lands 05:00 — Tourist Information counters may not be open yet; IC card machines are 24/7

**Cost:**
- ⚠ **PRICING NOT VERIFIED** — Regular Suica is ¥500 deposit + ¥1500 initial balance = ¥2000. Welcome Suica likely has no deposit, so expect ¥1500-2000 for the card with initial balance.

**For children:**
- ⚠ **CHILDREN'S WELCOME SUICA NOT CONFIRMED** — japan-guide.com mentions "cards for children" exist for regular IC cards (ages 6-11, half-fare), but did not specify if Welcome Suica has a children's version
- **LIKELY SOLUTION:** Buy adult Welcome Suica for the 6-year-old. You'll pay adult fares (not half-fare) but avoid registration hassle. For 12 days, the extra cost is minimal (~¥200-300 per trip vs ¥100-150 child fare)

## VERIFIED: Android / Google Wallet Suica

**Bad news for non-Japanese Android phones:**

❌ **Mobile Suica and PASMO apps are DIFFICULT OR IMPOSSIBLE for foreign tourists on Android.** 

Why:
- Requires **Osaifu-Keitai** (FeliCa chip) — only available in Japanese-market Android phones
- Requires Japanese address and Japanese credit card for registration
- Interface is Japanese-language only

**Exception coming in 2027:**
- Samsung reportedly plans to make new phone models Suica-compatible worldwide starting "first half of 2027" — too late for your July 2026 trip

**Android users: BUY PHYSICAL CARD at airport.**

## VERIFIED: One Card Per Person Rule

⚠ **EACH FAMILY MEMBER NEEDS THEIR OWN IC CARD.**

IC cards work by tapping in and out of train gates. You cannot tap one card four times for a family — the gate system tracks entry/exit per card. Even for kids.

So you need:
- Dad: Mobile Suica (iPhone) or physical card
- Mom: Mobile Suica (iPhone) or physical card  
- Kid 1 (~6yo): Physical Welcome Suica
- Kid 2: Physical Welcome Suica

## VERIFIED: What IC Cards CAN and CANNOT Be Used For

**CAN use for:**
- ✅ All trains: JR, Tokyo Metro, Osaka Metro, Kyoto buses (tap in/tap out)
- ✅ Convenience stores: 7-Eleven, Lawson, Family Mart (tap to pay)
- ✅ Vending machines (drinks, snacks)
- ✅ Some taxis (if marked with IC card reader)
- ✅ Coin lockers at major stations

**CANNOT use for:**
- ❌ Shinkansen reserved-seat tickets (you need your JR Pass + seat reservation)
- ❌ Some small rural buses/trains (cash only)
- ❌ Most restaurants (still predominantly cash or credit card)

(verified — Japan Guide, Nov 2024)

## How to Set Up eSIM (iPhone & Android)

### iPhone:

1. **Buy eSIM** from Airalo app or BNESIM/provider website (before you fly)
2. **Install eSIM:** You'll receive QR code or direct install link → Settings → Cellular → Add eSIM → Scan QR code or use app install
3. **Label it:** Name it "Japan Data" so you know which line is which
4. **Set as data line:** Settings → Cellular → Cellular Data → Select "Japan Data" eSIM
5. **Keep your Indian SIM active for calls/SMS** (Dual SIM works: Indian SIM for calls, Japan eSIM for data)
6. **Activate on arrival:** When you land, turn on Cellular Data for the Japan eSIM. It auto-connects to the network and starts your validity period.

### Android:

1. **Buy eSIM** from provider (Airalo, BNESIM, etc.)
2. **Install eSIM:** Settings → Network & Internet → SIMs → Add SIM → Download a SIM instead → Scan QR code or enter activation code
3. **Enable eSIM:** Toggle on the new eSIM
4. **Set as data SIM:** Settings → Network & Internet → SIMs → Select Japan eSIM for Mobile Data
5. **Activate on arrival:** Connect to network when you land

## Coordination Protocol If You Get Separated

**Tools you already have:**

1. **WhatsApp Live Location:**
   - Open family group chat → tap + → Location → Share Live Location
   - Choose duration (15 min / 1 hour / 8 hours)
   - Everyone in the group sees real-time location on a map
   - **Requires data connection** — this is why wife needs eSIM

2. **Google Maps Location Sharing:**
   - Google Maps → tap profile picture → Location sharing → Share location
   - Select family members → choose duration
   - More reliable than WhatsApp in low-signal areas

3. **iPhone Find My:**
   - If both parents have iPhones and are signed into Family Sharing
   - Settings → [your name] → Family Sharing → Location Sharing → Share My Location
   - Works even in low-data situations (uses Bluetooth mesh network)

4. **Pre-agreed meeting points:**
   - For each major location (DisneySea, USJ, Fushimi Inari, teamLab Borderless), agree on a "if we lose each other, meet here in 30 min" spot
