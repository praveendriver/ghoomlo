Paperwork + safety

Visa, insurance, health · before you go

Japan is one of the safest destinations on earth — but Indian passport holders need an eVisa, kid vaccines need a pediatrician sign-off, and travel insurance is genuinely worth the ₹3-5K. Don't skip these.

Japan eVisa (Indian passports)

Japan accepts eVisa applications from Indian citizens since 2024 — single-entry tourist eVisa is the standard. Apply 4 weeks before departure — average processing 5-10 working days. Apply for all 4 family members in one batch.

Cost: FREE for Indians (eVisa fee waived) Validity: 90 days from issue Stay: up to 30 days
Apply on MOFA eVisa portal Embassy India

Required: Yes (medical proof at immigration on request) Cost: ₹4-6K family / 13 days
