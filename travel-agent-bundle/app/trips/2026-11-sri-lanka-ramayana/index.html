November 2026 · Colombo · Kandy · Hill country

Old Colombo, Kandy,
and the Ramayana trail.

Two nights in Colombo around your Saturday conference — old Fort and Pettah on Friday, temples, legacy pure-veg eating houses and the best shopping on Saturday afternoon. Then up to Kandy for the Temple of the Tooth, and into the hill country for the places tradition links to Sita and Hanuman — the Ramboda Hanuman temple, the Seetha Amman temple and Hakgala, said to be Ashok Vatika. A trip for two — strictly lacto-vegetarian, unhurried, home Tuesday night.

The timeline

Colombo for two nights, then one night each in Kandy and Nuwara Eliya, then a long drive to the airport. Drive times are estimates (EST). The fast expressway does not reach Kandy yet, so the hill legs are slow roads — the times below already allow for that. Temple hours and fees that couldn't be checked are marked ⚠️ confirm, not invented. Strictly lacto-vegetarian throughout.

Sita · Hanuman · Vibhishana · Rama

The Ramayana trail

Sri Lanka Tourism runs an official “Ramayanaya” trail of about fifty sites. They are places of traditional belief, not proven history — treat them as pilgrimage, not archaeology. Four of them sit on this route. The rest are a long way off, so they are listed honestly below with why they were left out.

Hidden fish
Seeni sambol mix (has Maldive fish), ambul thiyal paste (fish), black pork curry paste, chilli pastes without a label, and “soya” or instant noodle packs — often chicken or seafood flavour.
Gems — the rules that protect you
Only buy with a lab certificate
Insist on a certificate from the government lab, the NGJA (National Gem & Jewellery Authority) — lab at No.12 Mackson's Tower, Alfred House Garden, Colombo 03. ⚠️ Saturday hours unknown, may be shut. Private lab: GIC, 115A Havelock Rd, +94 11 2506932.
⛔ Never buy where the driver takes you
Drivers often earn a commission from “gem museums” and spice gardens. We could not verify any retail gem house, so none is named here.
Keep it outside the trip budget
A stone is something you own, not a trip cost. Set its budget separately, keep the invoice and certificate, and declare it at Indian customs.
Eating safely · legacy eating houses

Vegetarian anchors to carry

Sri Lanka's hidden problem is Maldive fish (dried tuna flakes, called umbalakada). It goes into many “vegetable” curries and sambols. Egg hides in hoppers and kottu. So stick to the pure-veg Tamil eating houses below, and everywhere else say “no egg, no fish — umbalakada epah” (“no Maldive fish, please”). Pins for the Kandy and hill eateries are approximate.
