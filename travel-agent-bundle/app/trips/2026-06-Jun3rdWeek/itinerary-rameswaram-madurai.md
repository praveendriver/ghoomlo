# Itinerary — Rameswaram + Madurai
**Mon Jun 15 → Sun Jun 21, 2026 (7 days, 6 nights)** • Family of 4 (kids 6, 9) • Slow pace

## Pace rule
7-day trip → slow pace = 1 anchor activity per day + downtime. Heat-aware: anchor activities are early morning or late afternoon; mid-day = AC.

## Day 1 — Mon Jun 15 — Travel + Madurai arrival
- **Morning**: Pickup BLR T2 → flight BLR → IXM (recommend mid-morning IndiGo / AI Express, ~1h 15m).
- **Afternoon**: Arrive Madurai. Hotel check-in (Courtyard by Marriott — Titanium suite upgrade likely). Pool + lunch.
- **Evening (5:30 onward)**: Walk around the hotel; gentle intro. **No temple today** — let the kids settle.
- **Dinner**: Hotel restaurant or **Murugan Idli Shop** (legendary Madurai chain, kid-OK, ₹250-400/head).
- **Reservations needed**: Flight; airport pickup.

## Day 2 — Tue Jun 16 — Meenakshi Temple
- **5:30am**: Early start. Drive to **Meenakshi Amman Temple** for darshan when crowds are thinnest. Hire a temple guide (~₹500-800) — explains the 1000-pillar hall and Tamil iconography to the kids.
- **9:00am**: Back to hotel. Breakfast (free for Titanium).
- **Mid-day**: Pool, kid downtime.
- **4:30pm**: **Thirumalai Nayakkar Mahal** (17th-century palace) + sound-and-light show at 6:45pm (English narration available).
- **Dinner**: **The Sangam Restaurant** (multi-cuisine, kid menu) or hotel.
- **Reservations needed**: Temple guide (book day prior); palace tickets at gate.

## Day 3 — Wed Jun 17 — Madurai light day + transit prep
- **Morning**: Optional **Gandhi Memorial Museum** (well-curated, 1.5h, kid-OK) OR pool morning.
- **Late morning**: Pack, late checkout (Titanium = 4pm guaranteed).
- **Lunch**: **Konar Kadai** (heritage Tamil veg meals served on banana leaf, ~₹400/head) OR Pasumalai garden lunch at **Taj Gateway Pasumalai** (heritage hill property, scenic).
- **Afternoon (3pm)**: Madurai → Rameswaram by car (~4h, NH87). Pre-arrange a Toyota Innova / Crysta with driver (~₹4,500-6,000 one-way). Snacks + entertainment for the 6yo.
- **Evening**: Cross **Pamban Bridge** at sunset — the new road bridge has stunning views; old rail bridge is iconic.
- **Check-in**: **Hyatt Place Rameswaram**. Dinner at hotel; early sleep.
- **Reservations needed**: Car/driver; hotel.

## Day 4 — Thu Jun 18 — Ramanathaswamy + sacred bath
- **5:00am**: Drive to **Ramanathaswamy Temple** for early darshan and the **22-well sacred bath** ritual. Plan for ~3 hours including changing/drying. Hire a priest (~₹500-1,000) to navigate the wells if you want the full ritual experience. **Skip the bath if not comfortable** — darshan alone is the core.
- **9:30am**: Back to hotel. Big breakfast.
- **Mid-day**: Pool. Family rest.
- **5:00pm**: Sunset at **Agnitheertham** (sacred sea-bath area; gentle waves). Beach time for kids.
- **Dinner**: Hotel OR **Hotel Tamilnadu Rameswaram** (TTDC) for local Tamil dinner.
- **Reservations needed**: Priest contact (hotel concierge can arrange).

## Day 5 — Fri Jun 19 — Dhanushkodi + Sea World
- **6:30am**: Drive to **Dhanushkodi** (~18km, 30-40min). The "ghost town" + southernmost tip; where Sri Lanka is closest. Take a 4WD beach jeep last 5km (regular cars can't go further). Spend 2 hours.
- **10:30am**: Back via Pamban for breakfast/early lunch.
- **Mid-day**: Hotel rest.
- **4:00pm**: **Sea World Aquarium** (small but the only one in TN — kids love it). 1h.
- **5:30pm**: **APJ Abdul Kalam Memorial** (impressive design, well-presented, 1h). Kids 9yo will engage; 6yo less so but quick.
- **Dinner**: Local fish/vegetarian thali at hotel.

## Day 6 — Sat Jun 20 — Slow day + last temple visit
- **Morning**: Sleep in. Breakfast at hotel.
- **9:00am**: Optional second darshan at Ramanathaswamy (less crowded than weekday).
- **Mid-day**: Pool. Pack.
- **4:00pm**: **Gandhamadana Parvatham** — the highest point on the island; hilltop temple (Ramarpatham) with Rama's footprint imprint. Short climb, good viewpoint. 1h round-trip.
- **Sunset**: **Kothandaramaswamy Temple** at the southernmost point. Drive back through coastal stretch.
- **Dinner**: Special family dinner — book ahead at Hyatt's restaurant for a final-night feast.

## Day 7 — Sun Jun 21 — Return
- **Morning**: Slow check-out. Breakfast.
- **9:30am**: Depart Rameswaram by car → Madurai (4h).
- **2:00pm**: Lunch in Madurai. Optional: pick up **Sungudi sarees** or **brass** at Pudhu Mandapam.
- **Late afternoon**: IXM → BLR.
- **Evening**: Home.

## Why this trip works for kids 6 + 9
- Short flights (1h 15m each way) — no long-haul fatigue.
- Mix of temple + beach + aquarium + bridges + memorial — not all temples.
- Titanium hotel comfort (pool, free breakfast, kids' menu).
- Mostly horizontal terrain — no altitude or steep climbs.
- Cultural depth: Char Dham + Jyotirlinga + UNESCO-tier Meenakshi all in one trip.
