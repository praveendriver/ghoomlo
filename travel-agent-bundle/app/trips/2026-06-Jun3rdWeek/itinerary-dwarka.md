# Itinerary — Dwarka + Somnath
**Mon Jun 15 → Sun Jun 21, 2026 (7 days, 6 nights)** • Family of 4 (kids 6, 9) • Slow pace

## Heat caveat
- Dwarka June: 32-37°C, humidity 65%+, pre-monsoon storm risk.
- All outdoor activities must run **5:30am-9:30am** or **after 5pm**. Mid-day (10am-5pm) = AC + pool + rest.
- Carry oral rehydration salts (ORS), kid-safe sunscreen, broad hats. Hydration plan = 3L water/day per adult, 2L per kid.

## Routing decision
Two airport options:
- **Via Jamnagar (JGA, 131km from Dwarka)**: closer, but BLR has limited direct service — usually a connect via BOM (~5-6h total).
- **Via Ahmedabad (AMD, 378km from Dwarka)**: BLR direct ~2h, then a long ~7h drive. Fewer flight risks but a brutal day-1 for kids.

**Recommendation: JGA via BOM.** Saves a 7h car day. Slightly higher fare but better day-1 experience.

## Day 1 — Mon Jun 15 — Travel to Dwarka
- **Morning**: BLR → BOM (~2h IndiGo/AI). Layover in BOM Lounge (Plaza Premium / OneWorld Sapphire access).
- **Afternoon**: BOM → JGA (~1h 30m).
- **Late afternoon**: JGA → Dwarka by car (~2.5h, 131km on NH947). Pre-arranged Toyota Innova/Crysta.
- **Evening**: Check-in **The Fern Residency Dwarka** (4-star, best modern available). Light dinner at hotel. Sleep early.
- **Reservations**: Multi-leg flight (book as one ticket); car driver for entire week.

## Day 2 — Tue Jun 16 — Dwarkadhish Temple + Gomti Ghat
- **5:30am**: Walk to **Dwarkadhish (Jagat Mandir) Temple** for early darshan. The 5-storey 78m spire + intricate stone carvings. ~2-2.5h including aarti.
- **8:30am**: Breakfast at **Gomti Ghat** café area; gentle morning by the river.
- **10am**: Back to hotel. Pool + rest until 5pm.
- **5:30pm**: **Sudama Setu** footbridge to Panchkui island; sunset views over the Gomti meeting the Arabian Sea.
- **Dinner**: Hotel restaurant.

## Day 3 — Wed Jun 17 — Bet Dwarka + Nageshvara
- **5:30am**: Drive to **Sudarshan Setu** bridge. Cross to **Bet Dwarka** (the original Krishna residence). Visit Bet Dwarka temple complex. 2.5h round-trip.
- **9:30am**: Breakfast en route.
- **10:30am**: Drive to **Nageshvara Jyotirlinga** (~16km from Dwarka). One of the 12 Jyotirlingas. Smaller temple, peaceful, 45min visit.
- **12:00pm**: Back to hotel. Lunch + pool + long siesta.
- **5:30pm**: **Rukmini Devi Temple** (~2km from Dwarka, 12th century). 1h.
- **Dinner**: Hotel.

## Day 4 — Thu Jun 18 — Beach + slow day
- **6:00am**: **Shivrajpur Beach** (Blue Flag, 14.5km). One of India's cleanest beaches. Kid swim time, gentle waves. 2-3h before heat.
- **9:30am**: Breakfast at a beach café OR back to hotel.
- **Mid-day**: Pool + nap. Long break.
- **5:00pm**: **Lighthouse at Dwarka Point** (visible 16km out to sea). Climb at sunset for views.
- **Dinner**: Try **Sangam Family Restaurant** for local Gujarati thali (kid-OK, ₹350-500/head).

## Day 5 — Fri Jun 19 — Day trip OR move to Somnath
**Option 5a (Recommended)**: Day trip from Dwarka
- **6:00am**: Visit **Dwarkadhish** for second darshan (Friday morning often special).
- **Late morning**: Optional **submarine underwater tourism** if launched (announced for Dwarka — verify availability).
- **Afternoon**: Hotel pool + early dinner. Pack for tomorrow's drive.

**Option 5b**: Move to Somnath (235km, 4h drive — long day for kids)
- **5:30am drive**: Dwarka → Somnath via coast. Carry breakfast.
- **Late morning**: Check-in at **Somnath Hotel Lords Inn** or similar 4-star (₹6,500-8,500).
- **Afternoon**: Rest.
- **Evening**: **Somnath Temple** evening aarti (Jyotirlinga #12). Powerful experience.
- **Dinner**: Hotel.

## Day 6 — Sat Jun 20 — Final day
**If stayed in Dwarka (Option 5a)**:
- **Morning**: Hotel pool, last shopping (silver, sea-shell crafts at Dwarka markets).
- **Afternoon**: Pack. Drive Dwarka → Jamnagar (2.5h). Overnight at **Hotel President Jamnagar** (4-star, near airport, ~₹6,000) or comparable.
- **Dinner**: Hotel.

**If went to Somnath (Option 5b)**:
- **6:00am**: Final Somnath darshan.
- **Mid-morning**: Drive Somnath → Jamnagar (~5h via Junagadh).
- **Afternoon**: Jamnagar overnight.

## Day 7 — Sun Jun 21 — Return
- **Morning**: Breakfast. Check out.
- **Late morning**: JGA → BOM → BLR. Layover lounge.
- **Evening**: Home.

## Caveats vs Rameswaram option
- **No Marriott/Hilton/Hyatt 5-star in Dwarka** → Bonvoy Titanium gets you nothing. The 40K cert is unusable.
- **Connecting flights** instead of direct → 5-6h door-to-airport-arrival vs 1h 15m direct.
- **Hotter, more humid** than Rameswaram — heat-management is a daily logistics problem.
- **Less kid-anchor variety** — temples + beach + lighthouse vs Rameswaram's temple + beach + aquarium + memorial + ghost town + bridges.

## Why you'd still pick this
- **Krishna pilgrimage focus** — Dwarka is THE Krishna site.
- **Visiting the Char Dham + Jyotirlinga combo** in the western coast.
- **Family ties / personal significance** to Krishna devotion.
- **You haven't been before** and want to tick the western Char Dham off the list.
