Home → BLR T2
Mon Jun 15 · 06:30 dep · 08:00 arrival

Pre-book Uber Reserve 48h prior — Bangalore early-morning surge is brutal. Family of 4 + luggage = Uber XL or Ola Prime SUV. Allow 90 min for traffic + 90 min at airport before boarding.

Check before leaving
  • Aadhaar / passport for all 4 (originals + photocopies)
  • Hotel + flight confirmations on phone + 1 printed copy
  • Web check-in done T-1 day · seat selection confirmed
  • Wallet · ₹15K cash · cards · forex card not needed (domestic)
  • Power: phones + power bank ≤10K mAh in carry-on
Carry vs check
  • Carry-on: docs, electronics, ORS, kid snacks, change of clothes for 6yo
  • Checked: bulk clothes, toiletries, gifts
  • Each adult 15kg + each kid 15kg = 60kg checked allowance (IndiGo)

Health + safety considerations
Throughout
Heat (real risk)
  • Madurai 38°C peak / Rameswaram 32-34°C with sea breeze
  • ORS sachets daily for kids · 3L water/adult/day
  • Activities only 5:30-09:30 and after 17:00
  • Watch for signs of heat exhaustion in 6yo: lethargy, no urination 4hr+
Food + water
  • Hotel water + sealed bottles only · no street water
  • Eat at hotel breakfast + verified veg restaurants only
  • Skip street food for kids — adult discretion
  • Carry kid medicines: paracetamol syrup, ORS, anti-diarrhea
Temple safety
  • Crowds: keep kids on shoulders or in firm grip · pre-trip practice "stay together"
  • Footwear: lockers at major temples cost ₹10-50 — use them
  • Temple steps can be slippery wet; supervise the 6yo
  • Phone in front pocket only · pickpocket-aware
Emergency
  • Pediatric hospital Madurai: Apollo Speciality (24h)
  • Rameswaram: Government Hospital + private clinics on Beach Road
  • Pharmacy chains: Apollo + MedPlus everywhere
  • India emergency: 112 · Travel insurance papers in hand
