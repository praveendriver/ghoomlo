## Option B — Dwarka (Gujarat) + optional Somnath/Bet Dwarka

### Routing

- **Day 2**: Jamnagar → Dwarka by road (~2-2.5h, 131km). Settle in.

### Heat caveat
- Day temps 32-37°C with 65% humidity climbing. Pre-monsoon storms possible from mid-June onward.
- Outdoor temple visits feasible 5:30am-9:30am and after 5pm. Mid-day = AC + rest.
- Carry oral rehydration salts. Not the kind of trip you "rush" — your slow-pace default helps.

## Recommendation

**Rameswaram + Madurai** wins on every dimension that matters for this trip:
1. Better June weather profile for the 6-year-old.
2. Shorter, simpler flights (1h 15m direct vs connecting/driving).
3. Meaningful loyalty payoff (Titanium upgrades + cert candidacy).
4. More kid-anchor variety to break up temple-heavy days.
5. Both options give you a Char Dham + Jyotirlinga pilgrimage credit, but Rameswaram's Ramanathaswamy is *both* in one.

**Cost is similar; quality of experience is materially different.**
