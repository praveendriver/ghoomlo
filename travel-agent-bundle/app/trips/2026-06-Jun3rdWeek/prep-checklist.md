## Packing List (heat-aware, family of 4)

### Documents
- 4 × Aadhaar cards (kids' included) — temple entries / hotel check-in
- Hotel + flight confirmations (printed + on phone)
- Driving license (if either parent will drive)
- Travel insurance card

### Health & comfort
- ORS sachets ×20 — heat is real
- Paracetamol (kids' formulation + adult)
- Anti-diarrhea tablets
- Mosquito repellent (DEET 20% adult, picaridin for kids)
- Kid-safe SPF 50+ sunscreen
- Sun hats for all 4 (Dhanushkodi sun is brutal; Dwarka beach sun similar)
- Hand sanitizer + wet wipes (temples)
- Small first-aid kit

### Clothes
- **Temple-appropriate**: long pants for adults; covered shoulders. Some temples ask men to remove shirts (Rameswaram inner sanctum). Kids fine in normal clothes.
- Cotton kurtas / loose shirts (cooler than synthetic)
- 1 swim outfit per person (pools + Shivrajpur/Agnitheertham)
- 1 light rain layer (pre-monsoon storm risk in Dwarka esp.)
- Sandals you can quickly remove at temple entrances
- 1 evening "nice" outfit for hotel dinners

### Electronics
- Phones + chargers + 1 spare power bank (≤10,000 mAh, hand-baggage-OK)
- Universal 3-pin charger; standard Indian sockets fine
- Kid tablet pre-loaded with offline content (4h flight legs for Dwarka)
- Action cam / DSLR if you want temple/coastal shots (no flash inside sanctums)

### Kid-specific
- Comfort toy (6yo)
- Snack bag (granola bars, dry fruits — cuts hangry meltdowns at unfamiliar food)
- Motion sickness medicine (Avomine kids' dose) for the 6yo — long drives + boat to Bet Dwarka
- 1 "boredom kit" each (coloring book + simple toys + headphones)

### Pilgrimage-specific
- Brass/copper container if collecting holy water (Gomti, sacred wells)
- Cotton bag for prasad
- Small donation envelopes (₹100-500 denominations, easier than asking for change)

## On-trip habits to maintain
- 2L water/kid + 3L water/adult/day. Fill bottles at hotel; avoid street water.
- Eat at the hotel or at known clean restaurants. Skip street food (fine for adults; kid stomachs are fragile on heat-trip).
- Daily ORS dose if temperatures cross 35°C
- Photo + journal habit — kids 9 can write a 3-line "what I saw today" for trip memory; ties into post-trip log
