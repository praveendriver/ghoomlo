# Age Gates for Kids 6 and 9

Quick reference. When in doubt, check the operator's stated minimum age — never assume.

## Both kids OK (6 and 9)
- Easy hikes <5km, mostly flat
- Snorkel in calm bays (with vest)
- Cable cars, gondolas, funiculars
- Vehicle safari / wildlife drives
- Beach + tide pools
- Cooking classes (kid-friendly variant — pasta, sushi-rolling, baking)
- Most museums (limit to 90 min)
- Boat tours (calm water, with life jackets)
- Train journeys / scenic rail

## OK for the 9yo, supervised for the 6yo
- Ski / snowboard school (most resorts: ages 4+ for ski school)
- Bike tours on dedicated paths
- Kayaking flat water (tandem with adult)
- Caving / show caves with steps

## NOT for this trip
- Scuba diving (PADI Bubblemaker is age 8+; Open Water 10+; full courses 12+)
- White-water rafting class III+
- ATV / quad bike off-road
- Helicopter tours (often age 2+ but motion-sickness risk for the 6yo)
- Bungee / skydive (age 18+ anyway)
- Long horse rides on open trails (pony rides are fine)

## Weather + heat rules
- Avoid 11am-3pm outdoor anchors when forecast is >34°C.
- Always have a hydration plan; one of the adults carries water.

## Walk-distance tolerance
- Flat: 6yo OK up to ~3km/day spread out.
- Stairs / hills: cap at 1km cumulative.
- Stroller still useful at age 6 for a long sightseeing day — note hotels that lend strollers.
