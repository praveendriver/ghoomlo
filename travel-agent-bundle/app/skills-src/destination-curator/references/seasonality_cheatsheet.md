Quick lookup to seed scoring before doing live `web_search`. Verify with current sources for the actual `travel_window` year.

## Avoid (when possible)

| Month | Avoid | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Jun-Sep | Goa, Kerala, Mumbai | Monsoon |
| Jun-Aug | South Europe (peak heat + crowds + price) | Use shoulder months instead |
| Dec-Feb | Northern Europe (short days, expensive) | Unless explicitly chasing winter/aurora |
| Apr-Jun | Rajasthan, Delhi (peak heat) | Plan winter instead |

When `web_search` results conflict with this cheatsheet, prefer the live source.
